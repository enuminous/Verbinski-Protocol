# Ethics note

A branching-world search can tempt its designer into treating people as parameters. This repository rejects that interpretation.

For real decision-support systems:

- simulated people are not interchangeable with real people;
- model outputs are evidence, not authority;
- counterfactual rankings inherit the values and omissions of their objective functions;
- uncertainty should be carried forward rather than hidden;
- high-impact actions need independent human review;
- the system should preserve meaningful human veto and appeal mechanisms;
- no simulation can justify otherwise impermissible harm merely because a score improves.

The traveler in the narrative bears memory of failed branches. A real system should likewise preserve provenance: what was tried, what assumptions were used, and why a branch was preferred.
